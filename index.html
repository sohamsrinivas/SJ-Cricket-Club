Strake Jesuit Cricket Club

The student-run cricket club of Strake Jesuit College Preparatory in Houston, Texas. Founded by students, coached by students, and open to anyone willing to chase a ball in the Texas heat.

Come to a practice Meet the team

Cricket, deep in football country.

Strake Jesuit lives and breathes Friday night lights. We are the group that shows up Saturday morning with a bat, a bag of tape balls, and an argument about field placements that never really ends. The club started in 2024 when a few of us realized the pickup games we grew up playing in driveways and cul-de-sacs deserved a real home on campus.

Some of our members learned the game from grandparents over grainy broadcasts of India versus Pakistan. Others picked up a bat for the first time at one of our open practices and got hooked on the sound of a clean cover drive. Both kinds of players matter here. We teach the game from zero, and we play it like it's being broadcast to millions, even when the crowd is one groundskeeper and somebody's younger brother.

Off the field, we're building something that outlasts any single season: a club younger students inherit, a fixture list that grows every year, and a corner of campus where a very old game gets a very Texan welcome.

What we're here to do

Learn the game

Most Americans have never watched a single over of cricket. We fix that one member at a time: the rules, the technique, the history, and the vocabulary you need to argue an LBW call with confidence. No experience assumed, ever.

Play the game

Practices on campus, nets and fielding drills, intramural squads, and fixtures against other Houston-area sides. We send an email before every meeting with the time and place, so get on the list and show up ready to play.

Make it American

The long game is bigger than one school. We want cricket to be a normal sport in the US, the kind you can find on any campus, and that starts here: demo days, curious freshmen, and a bat handed to anyone who stops to watch.

The people running the show

Five officers, one shared group chat, zero paid staff. Everything this club does, from booking fields to teaching the forward defensive, happens because these people make it happen.

Fair questions

I've never played cricket. Can I still join?

Yes, and honestly you're our favorite kind of member. Most of the club learned here. We'll teach you the rules, the grip, and why everyone yells so much when the ball goes near the stumps.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. The club keeps bats, balls, pads, and gloves for everyone. If you fall in love with the game and want your own kit later, the officers have strong opinions and will gladly share them.

Will this conflict with my other sports or clubs?

We schedule around the big stuff on purpose. Plenty of members balance cricket with other teams and activities. Come when you can; nobody keeps an attendance ledger.

Is this real cricket or a casual thing?

Both. Practices are open and low-pressure, but our fixtures are real matches with real scorecards. You choose your own level of seriousness. The club has room for all of it.
